*This one was written while listening to Karibu-Orange Express by Sadao Watanabe*

I love serendipitous bs, I don’t know what to tell you, I believe some things are just meant to reach you, and I hope, my fellow reader, that this post is to you, what one tiktok was for me, I was mindlessly scrolling through the spying Algo and ran into the booktok part of it. The caption read: *“5 Books to read for Trans Visibility day.”* So I saved it, and then went back through the description less list, chose the first one, my friend, when I tell you I was throughly surprised by it... 

This book hits all the marks, Sci-fi, Space, a little bit of cyberpunk and a Heist, I felt like in that one Rick & Morty Episode where they recruit for the heist convention... you SOB I’m in. My dear reader, have you ever wondered *”What if Ocean’s 8 but in space?”* that’s what this book is, good story, good pace, good setting and yes a bit of smut. 

This one was really fun, and I would like to close this little post by saying Fuck you JK. Rowling you Terf cunt. 

B.
