Today was a fun one, I had a date planned but a series of unfortunate events led to having to take a rain check, so I took myself out for a date. 

Day’s like these are always fun, I get to take myself out and just chill, so I grabbed a book and headed for the door for a day of coffee, food and immaculate vibes. 

First stop was [Vissio Cafe](https://www.instagram.com/vissio.cafe/), it’s a small coffee shop with really nice beans, good baristas and a small selection of drinks, had the usual combo whenever I go to a new spot, a spro and a flat white, pro tip, if you ever happen to find yourself at a espresso focused coffee shop and you want to know if it’s good, have an espresso and a cortado, it’s the best way to get to know the kind of coffee they serve there, if both are good, then any kind of espresso based drink will be good.

## Funky

I mistimed my exit from lunch and got to [Funky](https://www.instagram.com/funkycafe.mx) right as they were about to close, grabbed a quick flat white to go, left a nice tip for the trouble and enjoyed my coffee on the go. Funky always has really nice beans, but today’s best drink was the flat white from Vissio, their signature blend is actually pretty nice with milk so they take the day.
