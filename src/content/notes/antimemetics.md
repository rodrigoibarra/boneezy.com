As I get older, it gets harder and harder to justify reading YA Novels which are extremely my shit, IDGAF, so it’s pretty cool whenever I find some “grown up” sci-fi. 

There is No Antimemetics Division began as a web series, and anything born on the web will always have a special place in my heart, so I was pretty excited for this one to come out. It’s heavy on existential dread and a little bit of horror, tho I kept having to go back a chapter or two whenever I put it down, just to get back into it.

> *”But if we have learned nothing else, we have learned this: humans can walk away from, and forget, anything. Civilization can go back to ‘normal’ after anything.”*

That one is a little bit on the nose given the current political climate.
